# Sourdough bread

Simple recipe, no knead. Takes about 24 hours from start to finish but only 20 minutes of active work.

## Ingredients

- 500g bread flour
- 350g water (room temp)
- 100g active starter
- 10g salt

## Steps

1. Mix flour, water, and starter. Rest 30 min (autolyse).
2. Add salt, fold into dough. Stretch and fold every 30 min for 2 hours.
3. Shape into a ball, place in a floured banneton. Cover and refrigerate overnight (12-18 hours).
4. Preheat oven to 250C with a Dutch oven inside.
5. Score the top, bake covered 20 min, then uncovered 20 min at 230C.
6. Cool completely before slicing (at least 1 hour).

## Notes

Last bake was slightly over-proofed. Try 14 hours max in the fridge next time.
